# 046: Payouts to centres and the platform fee (Razorpay Route)

**Status:** ready
**Mode:** solo
**Runs:** waits for 044, 045: needs billable attendance, consultation amounts and DoctorNearMe-collected payments

## Summary

**Business model (product owner, 2026-11-13, corrected):** the family pays on DoctorNearMe, and
DoctorNearMe pays the centre or therapist, keeping a platform fee per consultation.

The money moves with **Razorpay Route**, so funds stay in Razorpay's regulated escrow and never pass
through a DoctorNearMe bank account. Each centre has a Route **linked account**. When a consultation is
delivered (attended, or billable under the centre's policy), DoctorNearMe transfers
`consultation amount − platform fee − GST on the fee` from the family's payment to the centre.

Centres see every payout in a statement, and you see the platform revenue and any failures in admin.

## Functional requirements

- **FR-1** – Migration `<ts>_payouts.sql`:
  - `org_payout_accounts(org_id pk, razorpay_account_id null, status check in
    ('not_started','pending_kyc','active','suspended'), legal_name, updated_by, updated_at)`.
  - `platform_fee_policies(org_id pk, kind check in ('percent','flat'), percent_bps 0–3000 null,
    flat_paise null, min_paise default 0, cap_paise null, waived_until date null, version int,
    updated_by, updated_at)`. The platform default comes from `PLATFORM_FEE_DEFAULT_BPS`.
  - `fee_policy_acceptances(org_id, policy_version, accepted_by, accepted_at)`.
  - `payout_items(id, org_id, appointment_id unique, payment_id, consultation_paise, fee_paise,
    gst_paise, net_paise, status check in ('pending','transferred','on_hold','released','reversed',
    'failed'), razorpay_transfer_id null, failure_reason, created_at, updated_at)`.
  - `platform_invoices(id, org_id, period_month, fee_paise, gst_paise, invoice_no unique, pdf_path,
    issued_at)`.
  - Register all of them in the 041 privacy registry (financial retention: 8 years, per the CA's
    advice; record this as an open question).
- **FR-2 – Paid services need an active payout account.**
  - A provider in an org whose payout account is not `active` cannot publish `online` services or offer
    packages. The UI says "Finish payout setup to accept payments", and the API returns 409
    `payout_account_inactive`. Free services still work.
  - Existing paid services of such orgs are hidden from booking until the account is active.
  - Admin sets the account id and status by hand: KYC happens in the Razorpay dashboard, which is a
    human task. `DEMO_MODE` fakes an active account.
- **FR-3 – Payout per consultation.** When a consultation becomes billable (044) or a one-off appointment
  becomes `completed` (030), create exactly one `payout_item`:
  - fee = policy (percent of the consultation amount, or flat; clamped to min/cap; 0 while `waived_until`
    is in the future);
  - GST = fee × `PLATFORM_GST_BPS` (default 1800);
  - net = consultation − fee − GST.
  - An outbox job creates the Route **transfer from the payment** (`POST /payments/:id/transfers`, test
    mode) for `net` to the org's linked account. Several transfers can come from one package payment,
    one per session.
  - Signed, deduped webhooks `transfer.processed` and `transfer.failed` update the status. Failed
    transfers retry with backoff 3 times, then show in admin.
- **FR-4 – One-off bookings paid upfront (031).** The transfer is created when the appointment is
  completed. A provider no-show, or a cancellation that entitles the family to a refund, creates no
  transfer; the refund follows 031.
- **FR-5 – Refunds after a transfer.** Reverse the transfer first (`POST /transfers/:id/reversals`) for
  the refunded consultations, then refund the family. The payout item becomes `reversed`.
- **FR-6 – Package expiry.** When a package expires with unused sessions, the org setting
  `package_expiry_policy` decides what happens:
  - `pay_centre` (default): the unused sessions are paid out as if delivered, with the fee applied.
  - `refund_family`: the unused amount is refunded through the 045 refund path.
- **FR-7 – Centre view `/dashboard/payouts`** (owner/admin):
  - Payout account status with setup steps.
  - This week's and month's consultations, gross amount, platform fee and GST, and net paid out.
  - A list of payout items with status, plus a CSV export.
  - Monthly **platform fee tax invoices** (PDF, gap-free `DNM-INV/<FY>/<0001>`, the platform's GSTIN
    from env, the centre's GSTIN if given). They are generated by a cron job on the 1st (Asia/Kolkata).
- **FR-8 – Fee transparency.** Before an org's first paid service goes live, the owner sees the fee
  policy ("DoctorNearMe keeps X% of each consultation fee, plus GST, and pays the rest to your account
  after each session") and accepts it once per policy version.
  - Families never see the fee. They see the centre's price, paid to "DoctorNearMe on behalf of
    <centre>".
- **FR-9 – Admin `/admin/payouts`:**
  - Fee policies per org (with a new version on each change).
  - Payout accounts.
  - Failed and on-hold transfers with `Retry`.
  - Platform revenue this month (fees and GST), and gross value (GMV).
  - Every change is audited.
- **FR-10 – Reconciliation.** A daily job compares captured payments, payout items and refunds per org.
  It flags any payment where `transferred + refunded + platform share ≠ captured` once the related
  sessions are settled, and alerts admins through the outbox.

## Non-functional requirements

- **NFR-1** – Exactly one payout item per consultation under retries (a unique index plus idempotent
  jobs with a Razorpay idempotency key).
- **NFR-2** – Test keys only (031 D-1). Live mode is a separate ticket after CA and legal review.
- **NFR-3** – No transfer is ever larger than the remaining untransferred amount of its source payment
  (checked in SQL).

- Instant payouts.

## Decisions

| # | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|----------|----------|-----|
| D-1 | Who collects and who pays out | DoctorNearMe collects; Razorpay Route transfers to the centre | Product owner 2026-11-13 (corrected) |
| D-2 | When the centre is paid | After each delivered consultation | "Per consultation", and refunds stay simple |
| D-3 | Funds custody | Only inside Razorpay (Route); never through a DoctorNearMe bank account | Holding and paying out merchants' money ourselves would need an RBI payment aggregator licence |
| D-4 | Visibility | The fee is shown to centres, never to families | The fee is part of the centre's price |
| D-5 | Free pilot month | `waived_until` per org | PMF check §6 |

## Open questions (product owner / CA / legal)

- The pilot fee rate. Suggested: 8–10% of the consultation fee, or a flat ₹60–100 per session.
- GST on the platform fee, e-commerce operator obligations (TDS 194-O, GST s.52), invoice format, and
  record retention. Needs a CA.
- The Razorpay Route agreement and the marketplace terms for centres (legal).
