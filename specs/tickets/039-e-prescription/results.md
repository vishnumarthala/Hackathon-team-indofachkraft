## Deviations

- **Signature**: "signed" means an authenticated, server-checked click by a verified prescriber recorded with signer,
  timestamp, content hash and an immutable version (rule 9). No DSC / Aadhaar eSign (out of scope, legal review in the
  ticket's open questions).
- **PDF fonts**: `pdf-lib` standard Helvetica (WinAnsi). Characters outside it (Devanagari/Telugu names) print as `?`
  in the PDF; `content_md` keeps the original text. Embedding a Unicode font (with `@pdf-lib/fontkit`) is a follow-up.
- **Weight**: `child_snapshot.weight_kg` is supported by the document/PDF but not captured by the editor (no weight in
  the 021 health profile); left out for the pilot.
- **WhatsApp**: `prescription.signed` / `.voided` go in-app + email only (no approved WhatsApp template; the task note
  asked for in-app + email). 032's opt-in `no-tracking` live crawl currently fails on `/family/messages/[threadId]`
  (needs a thread; unrelated to this ticket); the new `/family/children/[id]/prescriptions` page passes it.
