Observations
Social relationship and reciprocity

Ravi did not look at people when spoken to and did not turn to his name in three attempts during the session. The mother reports that he plays alone and does not join his cousins. (items 1, 5, 6, 7, 9)

Speech-language and communication

The mother reports a few words at age 2 that were later lost; no words were heard in the session. He repeats lines from cartoons and does not point to request or to show. (items 15, 16, 18, 20)

Behaviour patterns

Hand flapping was seen twice during play. The mother reports lining up of toys, strong distress when the route to school changes, attachment to one toy car and hitting his own head when upset. (items 24, 25, 28, 29, 30)

Sensory aspects

The mother reports that Ravi covers his ears at the mixer and the pressure cooker whistle and sometimes hides. (items 31)

Red flags
Lost speech
Self-injury
Suggested next steps
Referral to specialist (Phase 3)
Discuss the reported loss of words with a developmental paediatrician.
Make a safety plan with the family for head hitting when Ravi is upset.
Keep daily routines predictable and prepare Ravi before changes such as a new route.
Offer a quiet space during loud kitchen sounds at home.
Review progress with the family at the next session.
